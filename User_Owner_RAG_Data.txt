1. El Narges Master Plan & Land Allocation
Category: Project (Master plans)
The El Narges Compound spans 45 acres in Al Rehab City, New Cairo. The development strictly adheres to an 18% low-density footprint, dedicating 82% of the total area to landscaped green parks, crystal water features, and pedestrian greenways. The residential zoning is divided into two distinct neighborhoods: The Villa Cluster (standalone villas and twin houses) and The Residences (G+3 luxury apartment buildings).

Category: Project (Developer specifications)

Any solar panel installations or rooftop pergolas must be pre-approved by the El Narges Architectural Review Committee (ARC) and must not exceed 2.5 meters in height.

16. FAQ: How does the FIFO Broker Interest Request system work?
Category: Company (FAQs)
The El Narges Portal operates a First-In, First-Out (FIFO) queue for broker unit reservations. When a broker submits an interest request for an available property (Status 1), the system locks the priority timestamp. If approved by sales administration, the unit transitions to Reserved (Status 2) for a 48-hour payment window. If the client fails to complete the booking deposit within this timeframe, the unit automatically reverts to Available.

22. Salary Affordability & Installment Suitability Guidelines
Category: Company (Payment plans)
To guide buyers on financial suitability, the El Narges compound applies a logical affordability rule: a customer's maximum monthly installment should not exceed 40% of their net monthly salary.
For example:
- If a customer's monthly salary is 20,000 EGP, their maximum monthly installment is 8,000 EGP.
- If a customer's monthly salary is 50,000 EGP, their maximum monthly installment is 20,000 EGP.
- If a customer's monthly salary is 100,000 EGP, their maximum monthly installment is 40,000 EGP.
- If a customer's monthly salary is 200,000 EGP, their maximum monthly installment is 80,000 EGP.

When a client states their monthly salary, the AI Agent must automatically:
1. Calculate their maximum affordable monthly installment (40% of the salary).
2. Review the live catalog of available properties and their prices.
3. Compute the monthly installment for each available property under the compound's payment plans:
   - Standard Plan: Down payment = 10%, remaining 90% divided over 8 years (96 months). Monthly installment = (Price * 0.90) / 96.
   - Extended Plan: Down payment = 15%, remaining 85% divided over 10 years (120 months). Monthly installment = (Price * 0.85) / 120.
   - Fast Track: Down payment = 40%, remaining 60% divided over 2 years (24 months). Monthly installment = (Price * 0.60) / 24.
   - Zero Entry: Down payment = 0%, remaining 100% divided over 5 years (60 months). Monthly installment = Price / 60.
4. Filter and select the suitable properties and the corresponding installment plans where the monthly installment is less than or equal to the client's max affordable installment.
5. If none of the available properties fit their salary, the agent should politely explain the math, show the closest/cheapest options, and state the minimum salary required for them.
